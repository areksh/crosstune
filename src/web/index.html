      Depth 1 adds artists similar to yours; depth 2 also adds artists similar to those, and so on. By default each artist keeps to its own top matches: one that already came up (one of yours, one already in a source playlist, or one found from another artist) uses up a place and is listed again, but added once. Tick the box above to take the next most similar artist instead -- more artists, but less similar ones. The walk stops after the number of similar artists you set; Qobuz lists about 70 for a well-known artist.

                    Include
                    Filters

                    Excluded artists are not checked at all. Releases matching a release term are not listed. Tracks matching a track term are left out of a new playlist. Terms match whole words, ignoring case; wrap one in slashes for a regular expression.

                    Releases already in your favourite albums are hidden, and so are reissues and remasters of older albums: a release labelled "Remastered", "Anniversary", "Expanded" or the like, or one the artist first released before this date.

                    Jobs

                    What crosstune does on a schedule: managed playlists, filled from a source (new releases, other playlists, rules over your library, a discovery radio) and kept tidy (duplicates, better copies, tracks that stopped playing), and linked playlists kept the same on several services.

                    Managed playlists run while crosstune serve runs, or whenever crosstune run-due runs from your system's scheduler (crosstune schedule print shows how). A pass missed while the computer was off runs once when it is back.

                    Linked playlists

                    Your playlists on different services, kept the same both ways. When their copies disagree nothing is written anywhere until you choose here.

                    Linking needs two connected music services. Connect another in Settings.

                    Tidy-ups: what keeps it clean

                    No service can swap a favourite: an upgraded or repaired favourite is added anew and the old one removed, so the service's app lists it as recently added. crosstune keeps the original date for its own smart playlists and duplicate checks.

                    Changes a tidy-up makes are in History and can be undone; one you put back yourself is left alone from then on.
