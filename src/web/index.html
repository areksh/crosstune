Playlist from artists

One artist per line. Each artist contributes its most popular tracks.

Filters

Terms match whole words, ignoring case: "live" skips "Live at Leeds" but not "Alive". Wrap a term in slashes for a regular expression, e.g. /remix|edit/. Excluded tracks are replaced by the artist's next most popular and listed unticked. Artist names match the whole performer name, ignoring case and accents: "Drake" skips Drake but not Nick Drake.

Playlist from albums

One album per line as "Artist - Album". Each album contributes all of its tracks.

Filters

Terms match whole words, ignoring case: "live" skips "Live at Leeds" but not "Alive". Wrap a term in slashes for a regular expression, e.g. /remix|edit/. Releases matching a release term are never picked. Tracks matching a track term or an artist, or on a disc not listed (e.g. "1" or "1,3-4"), start unticked. Artist names match the whole performer name, ignoring case and accents: "Drake" skips Drake but not Nick Drake.

Similar artists

Start from a list of artists or one of your playlists, and add the top tracks of the artists Qobuz lists as similar to them.

Depth 1 adds artists similar to yours; depth 2 also adds artists similar to those, and so on. By default each artist keeps to its own top matches: one that already came up (one of yours, one already in a source playlist, or one found from another artist) uses up a place and is listed again, but added once. Tick the box above to take the next most similar artist instead -- more artists, but less similar ones. The walk stops after the number of similar artists you set; Qobuz lists about 70 for a well-known artist.

Filters

Terms match whole words, ignoring case: "live" skips "Live at Leeds" but not "Alive". Wrap a term in slashes for a regular expression, e.g. /remix|edit/. Excluded tracks are replaced by the artist's next most popular and listed unticked. Artist names match the whole name, ignoring case and accents: "Drake" skips Drake but not Nick Drake. A similar artist matching one is left out, and the next most similar takes its place.
